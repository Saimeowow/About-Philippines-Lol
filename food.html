Popular Filipino Food

Filipino food is known for its delicious combination of sweet, salty, sour, and savory flavors. Filipino cuisine has been influenced by many cultures throughout history, creating a unique variety of dishes enjoyed by people across the Philippines.

Adobo

Adobo is one of the most popular Filipino dishes. It is usually made with chicken or pork cooked in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

Sinigang

Sinigang is a warm and sour soup commonly made with pork, shrimp, fish, or vegetables. Tamarind is often used to give the soup its signature sour flavor.

Lechon

Lechon is a whole roasted pig that is often served during celebrations, holidays, and special occasions. It is famous for its crispy skin and flavorful meat.

Pancit

Pancit is a popular Filipino noodle dish. There are many different types, and it is often cooked with vegetables, meat, seafood, and soy sauce.

Halo-Halo

Halo-halo is a popular Filipino dessert made with shaved ice, sweetened fruits, beans, jelly, and evaporated milk. It is especially refreshing on hot days.
