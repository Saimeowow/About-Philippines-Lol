Popular Places to Visit

The Philippines is home to many beautiful and exciting places, each offering its own unique culture, history, and natural beauty. From stunning beaches and breathtaking landscapes to historic cities and vibrant communities, these destinations showcase what makes the Philippines special. Here are some popular Filipino places worth discovering.

Palawan (El Nido and Coron)

Towering limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, and World War II shipwrecks make this an ideal spot for island hopping and diving.

Boracay

Powder-soft white sand, vibrant nightlife, and a wide range of water sports make this a world-famous tropical retreat.

Cebu (Moalboal and Kawasan Falls)

Multi-tiered waterfalls, canyoneering adventures, and massive sardine runs draw thrill-seekers year-round.

Bohol

Rolling green hills known as the Chocolate Hills, river cruises, and tiny native primates called tarsiers highlight this island.

Siargao

Palm-tree forests, rock pools, and the legendary Cloud 9 surf break make this the surfing capital of the country.

Batanes

Green rolling hills, stone houses, and dramatic coastal cliffs in the far north offer a quiet, scenic escape.

Baguio City

Known as the "Summer Capital," this cool mountain resort city provides a breezy break from the tropical heat.
